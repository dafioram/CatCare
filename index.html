Chessie & Bruno

Everything you need to keep two very opinionated cats fed, watered, and out of the pantry.

Which setup is it today?

Not sure? Check the upstairs main bedroom door — closed means separated, open means they've got the run of the house.

🥫 Wet food

  • Check the kitchen table first — a can sitting out means wet food's on the menu this visit
  • Twice a day: breakfast and dinner
  • Half a can per cat, per meal, with the spoon onto a little plate

Feed them in their own separate areas — nothing extra to coordinate.

Chessie eats fast, so she gets held back until Bruno's had his turn:

  1. Put Chessie's plate in the guest bathroom and close her in there
  2. Feed Bruno at his mat by the coffee station
  3. Wait until Bruno's eaten all or most of it — Chessie's done in about 20 seconds, Bruno can take a lot longer
  4. Once Bruno loses interest, let Chessie out

It's fine if she grabs a few of his leftovers on her way over — the point is just to give Bruno a head start.

🥣 Dry food

  • Both cats have automatic feeders — mostly hands-off
  • Peek in occasionally and top up if the level looks low

🥣 Dry food

  • Skip it entirely while they're together
  • Chessie will happily polish off Bruno's share too, given the chance

💧 Water & the fountain

  • Top off any bowls that look low
  • The kitchen fountain has a little viewing window — refill when the level drops below halfway
  • Fill it back up past the halfway mark, but stop before the circle's completely full
  • To refill, just pour water straight into the top

🚽 Litter

  • Chessie — her box is in the shower
  • Bruno — he's on the Litter-Robot in the basement (upkeep below)
More on Chessie's box

There should already be enough litter in there. Every few days, scoop the clumps into one of the green bags, then toss the bag in the trash.

🚽 Litter

  • Both cats share the Litter-Robot in the basement
  • No separate box needed in this setup

🤖 Litter-Robot upkeep

  • Keep litter filled to the gray circle line visible inside the globe
  • Left of the robot is a Tidy Cats container with the scooper bowl on-top, open it, scoop a few out, and drop them in
  • No need to spread it — the globe does that on its own as it rotates
Emptying the waste drawer

Every 5 days or so, pull the bottom drawer handle out to check how full it is. If it looks pretty full, empty it into the outside trash can and pop in a fresh bag.

⚠️ If it starts acting up
  • Yellow light blinking → press reset
  • Red light blinking → press reset
  • Globe stuck mid-turn → totally normal, it'll resume on its own after about a minute. Hit cycle if you don't want to wait it out
🍞 If Bruno's feeder gets stuck

Stuck open? Press the big button on the back to close it.

Heads up: this feeder's built to open only for Bruno, but Chessie is stubborn enough to muscle her way in too — don't be surprised if she manages it.

🧸 Good to know
  • Chessie is extremely food-motivated — keep pantry doors shut or she'll help herself to treats
  • Her favorite toy is the wand/fish-pole toy
  • If she goes missing, check under the dresser with the TV first
  • Bruno's a treat fan too
